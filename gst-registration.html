Common questions 6 answers
Is GST required below ₹20 Lakh turnover? +

No, up to ₹20L (services) or ₹40L (goods), unless you sell inter-state, on marketplaces, or export. Registering voluntarily u/s 25(3) lets B2B customers claim Input Tax Credit.

Can I use a home or parent’s address? +

Yes. Pick Family / Consent NOC under Premises, print the NOC from the document panel, and upload it with the owner’s electricity bill or tax receipt.

Do I need a current account first? +

No. Under CGST Rule 10A, Tab 8 (bank accounts) is optional at filing. You have 30 days after your GSTIN arrives to add it.

Do software and service exporters pay 18% GST? +

Exports paid in foreign exchange are zero-rated under Section 16 of the IGST Act. File Form GST RFD-11 (LUT) after your GSTIN arrives to bill overseas clients at 0%.

How fast is GSTIN granted under Rule 14A? +

With Aadhaar OTP e-KYC and Rule 14A (monthly B2B tax ≤ ₹2.5L), the ARN is issued at once and Form GST REG-06 usually follows in 3 to 7 working days.

How do you avoid REG-03 query notices? +

Before submission we check that the bill and NOC addresses match, strip characters the portal rejects from Udyam numbers, and cut your photo to under 100 KB.
