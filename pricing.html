Pricing FAQs

Common questions about plans & billing.

Which plan is best for a shop, restaurant, or GST-registered business? +

The Business (₹499/mo) plan is our most popular choice for shops, cafés, restaurants, and GST-registered businesses. It includes 300 AI bill scans/month, GST and bank reconciliation, inventory and staff payroll, and real-time profit analytics.

What happens if I run out of AI bill scans during the month? +

Manual bookkeeping is unlimited on all paid plans, so you can always record entries manually. If you need more AI camera scans, you can top up an Extra 100 AI scans pack for ₹49 anytime, or upgrade to the next plan tier.

Can I export my accounting data if I cancel my subscription? +

Yes. Your books and financial records belong to you. Data exports remain available even after cancellation for a defined access period, and records are retained in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Does AI Pro change my books or file returns without asking me? +

Never. AI Pro proactively prepares journal suggestions, flags duplicate bills or anomalies, and organizes workflows for you and your CA — but it never submits tax returns, alters approved books, or makes payments without your explicit authorization.
