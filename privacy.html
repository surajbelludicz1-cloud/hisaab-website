Website privacy notice

This notice covers the Hisaab.Guru promotional website. It is not a privacy policy for the mobile app or a description of how app business records are handled.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who operates this website

Hisaab.Guru is operated by Suraj Belludi, sole proprietor. For support or privacy enquiries, email hello@hisaab.guru.

Email enquiries

When you email hello@hisaab.guru, you supply your email address and the contents of your message so we can understand and respond to your enquiry. Please do not send financial documents, passwords or one-time passwords (OTPs).

Mailbox-provider and email-retention details have not yet been confirmed for this notice. No particular storage location or retention period is stated here.

Your browser preferences

The homepage saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage under hisaab-web-theme. It uses that choice to restore the theme when you return. This entry has no automatic expiry set by the website. You can remove it through your browser’s site-data settings; doing so resets the saved theme. Browser storage is separate from any app account or business records.

Fonts, styling and animation services

The homepage loads fonts from Google Fonts and styling and animation files from jsDelivr. Your browser connects to those services to request the files, which exposes connection information such as your IP address and browser request details to them.

For their handling of those requests, see Google’s privacy policy and jsDelivr’s privacy policy. These services have their own policies; this notice does not promise a particular processing location or retention period for their records.

Forms, payments and server records

The website pages currently provided here have no account sign-in, data-upload, enquiry or checkout form. They do not include an analytics script. This does not establish that the hosting service keeps no logs or adds no tracking. Hosting request logs, any hosting-added analytics and their retention settings have not yet been confirmed.

Scope and information still to be confirmed

This is a limited website notice. Hosting-provider details and log retention still need confirmation. An app privacy notice covering accounts, cloud services, business records, retention and deletion is not yet published here. Do not use this website notice as assurance that app data stays only on your device or that deleting an item permanently erases all copies.

You can clear the website’s saved theme through your browser settings. For questions about website data or requests concerning access, correction or deletion, email hello@hisaab.guru. Please describe your request without sending financial records or identity documents in the initial message. This notice does not promise account-wide app deletion or a specific response time.
