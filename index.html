Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about bill scanning, bookkeeping, and GST registration.

1. How does Hisaab.Guru automate daily bookkeeping? +

Take a photo of any purchase bill or sales invoice on your phone. Hisaab.Guru automatically extracts the line items, categorizes the expense, updates your ledger, and calculates your GST Input Tax Credit (ITC).

2. How does the Bill Scanner work with printed and thermal receipts? +

Point your phone camera at any invoice or receipt. Hisaab.Guru reads the supplier name, bill date, items, total amount, and GST tax split in under a second, and adds it straight to your expense records.

3. How can I register a new GST number (GSTIN) for my business? +

Click Start 30-Minute GST Registration on our website. Upload your PAN card, Aadhaar card, and electricity bill. Our platform prepares all 10 sections of Form GST REG-01 and formats your documents automatically for OTP submission.

4. What if my shop or business is at a home address in a family member’s name? +

Hisaab.Guru automatically generates a ready-to-print Family Consent Letter (NOC) in one click so you can use your residential or family-owned premises for GST registration.

5. Do I need a current bank account before applying for GST? +

No. Under CGST Rule 10A, you can apply for and receive your GSTIN first, and furnish your business bank account details within 30 days after your GST number is granted.

6. Is my business data safe and private? +

Yes. All your invoices, financial reports, and identity documents are protected with 256-bit encryption and are never shared with third-party marketers.
